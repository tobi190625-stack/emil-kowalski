# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme sections (Liquid + CSS + vanilla JS) for the store `vertic-store.myshopify.com`. Chosen by the user.

## Users

Young riders across the EU (roughly 18–30) who snowboard or ski and buy mountain apparel that also works as streetwear. They mostly arrive on their phones, often from social ads.

## Product Purpose

Vertic sells snowboard & ski apparel (hoodies, t-shirts, jackets, accessories) printed to order. The storefront should get a visitor from the first screen into the Men or Women range with zero friction. Success: every tap goes where it looks like it goes, first time.

## Positioning

Print-on-demand apparel from a small Czech brand: made to order, shipped within the EU, no warehouse stock.

## Operating Context

Operated from the Czech Republic; EU customers; delivery 5–15 business days; 14-day withdrawal right; faulty items replaced free (from the store's published policies).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Homepage structure is fixed by the owner: a half-screen banner, then Men and Women tiles side by side filling the rest of the first screen, then a newsletter.
- Collections are not built yet. Sections must link to merchant-chosen URLs set in the theme editor, not hard-coded collections.
- Previous site felt glitchy: taps not navigating, needing double taps. Every control must be a real link and respond on first tap.
- Big but minimal. The owner rejected a blue and yellow palette; keep it monochrome.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Vertic. Snowboard & ski apparel.

## Evidence on Hand

- Store policies (shipping, returns). No reviews, press, or customer counts exist — do not invent them.
- Banner and collection imagery: real Pexels photos (free commercial licence). The owner does not want AI-generated imagery.

## Product Principles

1. First tap works. No interaction may need a second try.
2. The imagery sells; the interface recedes.
3. Two choices, not twenty: Men or Women.
4. Mobile first, because that is where the audience is.
